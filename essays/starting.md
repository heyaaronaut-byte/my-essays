title: On Starting
date: July 2026

Most of what I learn doesn't come from finishing things. It comes from the part right before I start, when the thing is still just an idea and I have to decide whether it's worth the discomfort of being bad at it for a while.

This essay itself is an example. I've wanted a place to write down what I'm learning for a long time, but "build a personal essay site" sat in the back of my mind as a vague, slightly intimidating project. It turned out to take an afternoon.

I think the gap between wanting to do something and actually doing it is usually smaller than it feels. The feeling is doing a lot of unearned work.

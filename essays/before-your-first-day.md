title: Before Your First Day
date: August 2026

August 31st 2026

(This is based on my first day of classes for Sophomore Year at Babson)Before you start your first day of anything (job, school, etc.), take a day or two off for yourself. Seriously, even though you may or may not be excited for the new environment, take a day off. 

Never thought that it would make a difference, but understand that more than not being in a calm mental state will do more for you than you can imagine. I’ve always thought that the idea of hitting the ground running meant doing everything in preparation to set yourself up for success. But sometimes, the best preparation you can do is mental preparation (staying calm and composed). Not organizing your schedule, not spending hours upon hours researching about x, and definitely not starting on work that you think you’ll do. Just rest. I know this might be counter intuitive to understand, but I think this was a mistake that I made where I didn’t take a solo day just to myself. If you’re probably always moving, building, and just in general doing things you might think “does that mean I do nothing then?”Not necessarily, if you consider your rest as sleeping, by all means go do that. What I am saying is that you should try to do a hobby you enjoy doing (reading, going for walks, painting, etc.). In essence, do something that grounds you to the present so that you are in a good mental state.Finding a hobby is another conversation, but in short if you’re trying to find a new “hobby” ask yourself this question I frequently ask myself: is there anything that I would regret trying or have wanted to try for a while but never had the opportunity to do so. And let that guide the conversations you have with yourself about finding something that grounds you in the present. 

So next time when you have your “first day” of anything, take a rest cause your body will thank you later. For me next time, looks like taking a mental rest (if not a day at least a couple of hours before bed to reset and ground myself by reading or anything of that sort).

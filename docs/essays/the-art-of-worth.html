The Art Of Worth

September 2026

What’s worth pursuing and working on?

A question that’s been on my mind a lot.

At times it feels easy to say “oh let’s pursue finance because there’s just a linear and direct route,” but internally it feels like it’s a different story.

You know that you can’t or won’t be satisfied if you took that “path”. I know it, you know it, and in all honesty you’re lying to nobody but yourself by saying that you’ll do that path even understanding the fact that you won’t enjoy it regardless.

And I guess that’s the thing about life, you never know what you’ll eventually end up doing unless you try and just shoot your shot.

I guess that’s the part that’s been holding me back, not knowing where to shoot my shot. If you’re a Type A or in essence and overachiever, you might very well feel the same way.

The feeling of not knowing what to pursue. For me it’s hearing all my friends and classmates say IB, GTM, and all of that, but deep down I know that I wouldn’t enjoy that. And I think that’s the hardest truth, knowing that you won’t enjoy something while also understanding that there’s another path, it’s just that you don’t know how or where to start.

Called my best friend earlier talking about what “success” means to each of us and honestly I don’t think even have an answer for that. My thinking is that success this year means working at a startup in one form and just providing value, yet I feel like there isn’t much value that I can really provide.

I don’t have a skillset in GTM, spitting out evaluations, nor copywriting in general.

Which got me thinking, maybe getting into copywriting might be worth it to try?

I don’t expect much, but at the very least it means something right…

This is what’s going through my head live and it’s interesting to write this out as I think, so maybe the answer to finding what’s worth it is to really ask ourselves: what’s a skill that I would enjoy try learning?
